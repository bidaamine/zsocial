# AI HR, Talent + Employee Intelligence


> This folder is intentionally documentation-only for now. It defines ownership, responsibilities, data flow, and future implementation boundaries. No application code is included in this generated structure.



Corporate AI for recruitment, screening, onboarding, performance, wellbeing, retention, and organizational health.

## Responsibilities

- Talent sourcing from platform and web signals.
- Bias-mitigated screening.
- Explainable candidate scoring.
- AI assessments for skills, culture fit, and potential.
- Personalized onboarding plans.
- Continuous review and development planning.
- Flight-risk radar.
- Employee wellbeing and culture intelligence.
- Succession planning and organization design insights.

## Safety

Hiring AI must include fairness constraints, explainability, bias audits, and human override with accountability.
