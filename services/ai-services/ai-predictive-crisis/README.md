# Predictive Crisis AI


> This folder is intentionally documentation-only for now. It defines ownership, responsibilities, data flow, and future implementation boundaries. No application code is included in this generated structure.



Early-warning system for personal and business crises.

## Crisis categories

- Health crises: physiological and behavioral precursors to medical issues.
- Personal life crises: financial, relational, emotional, and lifestyle disruption patterns.
- Business crises: operational, financial, market, and talent risks.

## Responsibilities

- Detect multi-signal patterns early.
- Estimate time horizon and severity.
- Recommend mitigation actions.
- Escalate to human professionals or internal safety teams when necessary.
- Maintain explainable risk reasons.
