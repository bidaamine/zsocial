# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — MVP Core, Months 1–6

Focus: prove the value.

Deliver:

- Personal AI Core.
- Life Orchestrator v1.
- Health Advisor basic.
- Education AI MVP.
- Family Hub basic.
- Social/family core.
- Corporate profiles.
- Basic brand builder.
- HR recruitment MVP.
- Web and iOS launch.

Target ideas from the product document:

- 10,000 invited beta users.
- Three user archetypes: families with children, young professionals, SMB owners.
- Early corporate customers.
- Series A readiness after beta evidence.

## Phase 2 — Full Product, Months 7–18

Focus: scale, monetize, expand.

Deliver:

- Digital Twin v1.
- Emotional AI Engine.
- Predictive Crisis AI.
- Full corporate suite.
- Campaign engine.
- Finance AI hub.
- Operations Centre.
- Android launch.
- Public API and developer ecosystem.
- Expansion to selected high-value markets.

## Phase 3 — Full Ecosystem, Months 19–36

Focus: ecosystem lock-in and global scale.

Deliver:

- AR/VR layer.
- Generational Memory full version.
- Collective Intelligence at scale.
- 50-language support.
- Global expansion.
- IPO-ready infrastructure.
