# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Beachhead segments

1. Parents of school-age children aged 6–16.
2. Health-conscious professionals aged 25–40.
3. Founder-led SMBs with 10–50 employees.

## Community-led growth

Launch communities before product release:

- Parents Community.
- Health & Wellness Community.
- Founders Community.

Use co-design sessions, founding-member pricing, and early access.

## Creator partner program

Recruit health, education, and productivity creators as real users, not generic endorsers.

## B2B sales motion

Start with product-led sales. The free corporate tier should let an SMB founder experience value before converting the whole team.

## Viral loops

- Family loop: one parent invites family.
- Workplace loop: one employee leads to team/company adoption.
- B2B network loop: one company invites suppliers, partners, and clients.
