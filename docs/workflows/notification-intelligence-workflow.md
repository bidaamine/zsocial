# Notification Intelligence Workflow

NEXUS notifications should be context-aware and emotionally intelligent.

## Flow

```text
New notification candidate
        ↓
Classify type: AI action, alert, insight, information
        ↓
Classify urgency and safety level
        ↓
Check user emotional state and current activity
        ↓
Check focus blocks, sleep, calendar, family safety, health urgency
        ↓
Deliver immediately, delay, bundle, suppress, or escalate
        ↓
Record reason in notification health score
```

## Priority order

1. Child safety and family emergency.
2. Health emergency or crisis escalation.
3. Security/account risk.
4. Time-sensitive user-authorized AI action.
5. Work/corporate critical alert.
6. Personal insight.
7. Informational update.
