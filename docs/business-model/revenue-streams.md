# Business Model and Revenue Streams

## 1. Personal subscriptions

- Free tier: useful basic product, AI social feed, health tips, limited education.
- Plus plan: advanced personal intelligence.
- Family plan: household-level value and lower churn.
- Elite plan: advanced AI, AR/VR, and Digital Twin capabilities.

## 2. Corporate subscriptions

- Starter for small businesses.
- Growth for SMBs.
- Scale for larger companies.
- Enterprise custom plans.

## 3. AI Marketplace

Third-party developers create specialized agents and tools. NEXUS takes a platform commission.

## 4. Data intelligence products

Only privacy-preserving, anonymized, aggregated insights. No individual data and no re-identification risk.

## 5. Professional services

Enterprise implementation, integrations, training, custom AI model adaptation, and consulting.

## 6. Premium AI and usage-based pricing

Advanced Digital Twin compute, AR/VR rendering, high-volume API calls, and custom model training.

## Strategic rule

No advertising revenue. The product should work for users, not sell their attention.
